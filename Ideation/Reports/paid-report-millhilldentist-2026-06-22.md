## Recommended Service

### Invisalign Landing-Page SEO Optimisation

Position the service as:

> A focused optimisation of the existing Invisalign page to improve its alignment with relevant London and North London Invisalign searches, clarify the patient decision journey and strengthen internal signals.

This is not a promise to reach page one. It is a practical project based on an existing page and verified ranking headroom.

## Suggested Scope of Work

### 1. Search-intent and keyword mapping

- Map relevant Invisalign searches to the existing page.
- Separate useful local and treatment intent from unsuitable terms.
- Do not position the practice as “cheap” unless that reflects its real offer.

### 2. On-page SEO recommendations

- Review the title, meta description and H1/H2 structure.
- Clarify London, North London and near-me relevance where appropriate.
- Review whether key patient questions are easy to find.
- Ensure clinical and pricing claims remain accurate and approved.

### 3. Content refinement brief

- Improve the structure of the existing long-form page.
- Reduce unnecessary repetition.
- Make costs, suitability, treatment process and next steps easy to scan.
- Add or refine FAQs only where they support observed search intent.

### 4. Internal-link plan

- Identify relevant homepage and treatment-page links into the Invisalign page.
- Use descriptive anchors without stuffing keywords.
- Keep the user journey to consultation or booking clear.

### 5. Conversion alignment

- Review the page-level call to action.
- Preserve the existing visible `Online Booking` route.
- Make the Invisalign-specific next step clearer for visitors evaluating treatment.

### 6. Implementation checklist

Deliver a prioritised document containing:

- Recommended metadata
- Heading changes
- Content-section changes
- FAQ recommendations
- Internal links
- CTA recommendations
- Measurement checklist
